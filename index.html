FAQ
What exactly is an ATS check?
Most companies run applications through Applicant Tracking Software before a human reads them. We screen your letter the same way — keyword coverage, quantified results, structure, length, clichés, and contact info — and tell you exactly what would get filtered out.
Is my cover letter shared or used for training?
No. Your text is used only to generate your analysis and is stored in your private history, which you can delete anytime. Share links contain scores only — never your letter text.
Do I need the job description?
It's optional but strongly recommended. With the actual posting, we match your letter requirement-by-requirement and your keyword analysis becomes far more accurate than generic role expectations.
What's free and what's paid?
Free: 3 analyses/month with the full report — ATS check, JD matching, rewrites, and revision tracking. Starter ($9/mo): 10 analyses/month plus Company Intelligence, which folds your target companies' latest news into the feedback.
